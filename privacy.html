Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CraftFlowPublisher ("the App", "we") handles information when it connects to Pinterest. The App is a personal, self-hosted tool operated by CraftFlowPublisher and is used only with the operator's own Pinterest account.

1. Information we access

When you authorize the App through Pinterest OAuth, it may access, only for the authorized account:

  • Basic account information (username, account type, profile details).
  • Boards and board sections.
  • Pins, including those created by the App.
  • Pin and account analytics (e.g. impressions, saves, clicks).

The App does not access other Pinterest users' private data and does not collect passwords.

2. How we use information

  • To create, schedule and publish pins to the authorized account.
  • To display boards so a destination for each pin can be chosen.
  • To show performance reports for the authorized account's own pins.

We do not use Pinterest data for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored only on the operator's private server (a Docker container) and is not uploaded to third-party services. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored securely and are never shared. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share Pinterest data with any third party. Data is sent only to Pinterest via its official API in order to perform the actions described above.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed to operate the App. You can revoke the App's access at any time in Pinterest under Settings → Security & logins → Apps. After access is revoked, or on request, stored tokens and cached Pinterest data are deleted within 30 days.

6. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, and to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
